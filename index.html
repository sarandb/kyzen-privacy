Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026 · App: KYZEN (Android) · Developer: SARAN · Contact: kyzen.db@gmail.com

KYZEN is a free workout, calorie and food tracker. This page explains what the app stores, where it is kept, and how you can delete it. In short: your data stays on your phone unless you choose to sign in with Google for cloud backup, and we never sell or share it.

What the app stores

DataWhyWhere it is kept
Name, age, sex, height, weight, goal, activity level, training daysTo work out your BMI, daily calories and workout planOn your phone. In the cloud only if you turn on backup.
Workouts: exercises, sets, weights, reps, times, caloriesYour training history, streaks and progressOn your phone. In the cloud only if you turn on backup.
Food log and custom foodsYour daily calorie and nutrition trackingOn your phone. In the cloud only if you turn on backup.
Equipment photos you takeTo show your own machines in the appOn your phone only. Photos are never uploaded.

The app uses the camera only when you choose to photograph equipment. It does not use your location, contacts or microphone, and it does not show ads or use advertising IDs.

Cloud backup (optional)

If you sign in with Google, a copy of your profile, workouts, equipment list and food log is stored in Google Cloud Firestore, linked to your account, so you can restore it on a new phone. It is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and only your signed-in account can read it. These services are run by Google (Firebase); see Firebase's privacy information.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or share it with anyone else.
  • We don't use your data for advertising.
  • We don't read your backup except when you contact us and ask for help with it.

How to delete your data

In the app: open the Profile tab, go to Account & backup, and tap Delete my cloud data & account. This permanently deletes your cloud backup and your KYZEN account straight away.

Without the app: email kyzen.db@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my KYZEN data", from the Google email you signed in with. We delete your cloud backup and account within 30 days and reply to confirm. You don't need to reinstall the app.

Data on your phone: uninstall KYZEN, or go to your phone's Settings → Apps → KYZEN → Storage → Clear storage.

When you delete your account, everything in your cloud backup is removed. We keep no copies.

Children

KYZEN is meant for adults (18 and over). It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Health note

Calories, BMI and plans in KYZEN are estimates for general fitness. They are not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new diet or training programme.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (for example, if ads are added in a future version), we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
